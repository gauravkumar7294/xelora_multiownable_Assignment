The files under contracts/zeppelin are minimal, compatible re-typings of the
OpenZeppelin (zeppelin-solidity) 1.9.x contracts used by xelora-contract, so the
project's original imports ("../zeppelin/contracts/...") resolve unchanged and
no network access is needed to fetch them. OpenZeppelin is MIT licensed
(c) 2016 Smart Contract Solutions, Inc.
For a production audit, replace them with the official package pinned to the
exact version originally used by the project.
